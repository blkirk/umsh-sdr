Desense is how far external noise raises an SX1262's own floor (thermal -117 dBm + 6 dB NF = -111 dBm in 500 kHz). It comes from the antenna floor's excess over the SDR's terminated-input floor, assuming the SDR and SX1262 have similar noise figures. Every dB of desense is a dB of link margin lost. Node-referenced dBm figures come from the node's RSSI through a different antenna, so treat them as approximate (±6 dB).
